# OfferAnchor: Business, Distribution and Impact

Baseline **2.2**; scope revision **11 October 2026**; original planning date **5 October 2026**. Communication owns this plan; DASE reviews measurements. These are hypotheses and decisions, not paying-customer, partnership, adoption or prevented-loss evidence.

## Product and first reachable audience

OfferAnchor helps Hong Kong jobseekers, part-time applicants and newly onboarded workers understand a new recruitment/onboarding instruction before using personal money, taking personal debt or handing over bank-account control. The first result is an evidence-linked action explanation and a short independent-verification question. An expanded editable brief supports review with a trusted person.

The initial audience is recent internship/part-time applicants reachable through teammates' classmates. Their availability makes a fictional pilot feasible; it does not prove that they face scams, represent Hong Kong students or want this product. Local and non-local students are included without treating origin as a risk factor.

The broader audience is intended eligibility, not validated demand or a new secured sales channel. Students, recent graduates and early-career applicants remain the initial reachable cohort. Campus pilot distribution and institution/grant sponsorship hypotheses remain unchanged; later worker-support/NGO channels require actual discovery and permission. Police case totals are not customer counts, unique users or willingness-to-pay evidence. PCPD construction-worker complaints support an adjacent privacy problem but not an established privacy feature or student market size.

## Distribution at the instruction checkpoint

The proposed trigger is: a new instruction asks for unfamiliar payment, borrowing or account access, or appears inconsistent with the role. A browser link offers a selected-message check. Only the latest instruction is required; advertisement/offer context is optional. No app installation, login, inbox connection or full conversation export is a P0 prerequisite.

The initial message is: **Understand what this job asks you to do before using your own resources.** Show one useful question and supporting evidence, with a company-funded/prohibition contrast. Fear, a fraud score or a claim to certify a company is not the value promise.

First pilot channel: consenting classmates/recent applicants, through team-controlled invitations and a link. Career resources, societies and campus onboarding guidance are later proposed placements requiring permission and demonstrated audience access. They are not secured distribution agreements. This documentation does not send invitations or authorize external publishing.

Before explaining the interface, run the fictional what-next observation in [user-research.md](user-research.md). Record initial choice, voluntary use when a tool is offered, intake time, help and abandonment. Describe these as task observations; laboratory availability is not real-world discovery or adoption. Ask whether an exported fictional brief is understandable to an independent recipient; a peer's feedback is usability evidence, not professional validation.

## Why a dedicated product might be worth using

The testable difference is reduced effort or fewer important omissions when preparing an independent question. Multi-message input, quoted reports, privacy notices and next steps are already available elsewhere. [Competitor evidence](research-and-sources.md) defines this boundary.

The dedicated workflow combines actor/resource/polarity/promise interpretation, exact passage inspection, corrections, preserved contradictions and application-owned routes. General AI can produce similar analysis. The mandatory comparison is the strong same-resource general-AI condition in [evaluation.md](evaluation.md), preferably using the same base provider/model. If it is equally useful with less effort, narrow the claim and reassess expansion; extra agent tools are not a remedy for absent user value.

Educational cases and Learn exercises may help users understand the checkpoint before they face a suspicious message. This is a testable distribution/learning hypothesis, not measured retention or a requirement to copy competitor content. Educational coverage and automated analysis coverage must be labelled separately; reading a privacy lesson does not activate EXT-01.

## Funding route and buyer hypothesis

Proposed progression: free small student pilot -> evidence discussion with a career/student-support service -> limited institution/grant-funded pilot if a need, responsibility and procurement route are established -> service agreement only after cost/privacy/support review.

A prospective buyer might fund reviewed guidance, a repeatable intake workflow, support and evaluation. Adviser time savings, recurring usage and budget are unmeasured. Campus anti-scam activity supports relevance, not willingness to pay. No price or institutional integration is promised.

Exclude loan/lead commissions, selling evidence, distress marketing, intrusive advertising and paid safe-employer certification. A small free trial is a learning phase, not a permanent funding model or insurance promise.

## Unit costs and cost controls

Cost per completed brief equals all model-call charges, including failed calls and retries, plus allocated hosting/protection and content-review/support costs, divided by completed briefs. Report usage, tariff/date/currency, selected provider/model/version, failure charges, hosting plan and human review time. Trial credits are temporary subsidies. Future operating estimates should disclose unpaid teammate work rather than silently treating maintenance as free.

The account owner/team must confirm spending limits, concurrency/rate controls and a working kill switch. This plan authorizes no purchase or billable subscription. Provider and host handling are separate gates in [privacy-and-safety.md](privacy-and-safety.md) and [setup-and-operations.md](setup-and-operations.md).

## Impact funnel and decisions

Keep separate: people reached in the actual named cohort -> checks initiated -> validated usable questions/briefs -> independent contact attempted -> user-reported review outcome. Measure only observed stages. A warning, exported brief, opened route or fictional task cannot be reported as a scam prevented or funds saved.

SDG 8 contribution is a proposed improvement in safer work entry and transparent recruitment instructions. Pilot reach should come from a concrete channel and observed response, not police percentages multiplied by the student population.

Decision gates before expansion:

1. Students initiate and finish the fictional workflow with acceptable effort.
2. The live workflow preserves key semantic controls and has no tested dangerous advice.
3. Compared with strong general AI, an observed benefit exists or the tie/limitation is reported.
4. An independent recipient can understand the exported selected-evidence brief.
5. A prospective sponsor articulates a need, review owner, procurement route and budget.

Items 1-4 are product-validation priorities; item 5 is later commercial discovery. Interviews remain pending. No current document establishes that any gate has passed.
