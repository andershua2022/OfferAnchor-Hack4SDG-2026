## Runnable demonstration sequence

1. Identify every scenario as fictional/source-inspired and display the actual live or simulation mode.
2. Start with the latest instruction only. Show that the student can obtain a useful action explanation and short application-owned independent-verification question without reconstructing an entire chat.
3. Add optional advertisement/offer context to reveal the duty/financial relationship. Inspect the exact current supporting passage; distinguish early commissions and repayment assertions from independent confirmation.
4. Copy the short question or preview the expanded brief. The exported text includes its locked provenance, uncertainty and claim-boundary footer.
5. Switch to the matched company-funded/prohibited-payment control. Negation and payer change the interpretation; there is no green safe-employer badge.
6. If time permits, add the authored contradictory reply. Earlier supported financial demands remain visible; a later denial or reassurance does not erase them.
7. Keep personal borrowing as a separate secondary mechanism for Q&A or the supplementary demonstration.

A literal obvious loan request alone is insufficient evidence of GenAI depth. The technical contribution is varying contextual actor/polarity/resource/mechanism/promise interpretation; exact quote matching is a separate engineering control, not proof of correct meaning. Corrections and uncertainty handling are part of the demonstrated workflow.

### A. Lead with the workflow improvement

The timed main pitch should explain the improvement over **traditional checking methods**, without volunteering the names of the closest commercial products. Organize this introduction around the user's task, not the age of a vendor or a list of products.

Use three narrowly described reference workflows:

- **A static warning/checklist used alone:** the user must interpret a particular instruction and apply general advice themselves. OfferAnchor structures the selected requirement into who acts, the money/account involved and supporting wording.
- **An identifier lookup used alone:** the user checks a number, address or URL within that service's coverage. OfferAnchor instead explains the financial request in the submitted words and prepares an independent-verification question. Both activities can be useful; an explanation is not a replacement for an official database.
- **Manual comparison and note preparation:** the user assembles earlier messages, later replies and an adviser question. OfferAnchor's planned/tested workflow preserves labelled sources, makes changes and unverified promises inspectable, and assembles a current evidence-linked record.

These are specific modes of use, not descriptions of every older product. In particular, do not describe Scameter+ as only a number checker: its published capabilities include AI screenshot analysis. Do not equate "traditional" with "obsolete", "no AI" or "ineffective".

The improvement to emphasize is **from generic warning or isolated lookup to a guided, inspectable request-to-question workflow**. Say what is added and demonstrate it. "Much more accurate", "dramatically faster", "safer than existing products", "first", and percentage/financial-impact claims require the matching completed comparison; a feature list does not establish them.

### D. Answers to rehearse

**"Isn't this already available in ScamOffer?"**

> Yes. ScamOffer already offers useful quoted warnings, patterns and next steps. Those features are not our exclusivity claim. We focus on Hong Kong recruitment and onboarding financial requests: who must pay or borrow, whether repayment is only a promise, and what a later reply changes. Our contribution is the bounded interpretation and correction-to-verification-record workflow, assessed with normal and concerning controls.

If the judge points out another genuine overlap, acknowledge it and return to the implemented task and evidence. Do not invent a missing competitor feature. Show an actual same-input comparison if available; otherwise call any performance advantage unmeasured.

**"Why not just use a strong general model?"**

> A strong model can interpret these messages, and we use one too. The product adds a defined workflow: separate sources, inspect the supporting passage, correct the reading, preserve later replies, and prepare a current verification record. We compare the completed task against strong general AI rather than assuming that an extra interface makes the model smarter.

**"Are you much better than older tools?"**

> We add contextual interpretation and record preparation to the narrower checklist or lookup workflow shown here. That is the specific improvement we demonstrate. A claim about higher accuracy or lower effort must use the actual comparison results and their scope.

**"Did you copy the library or learn from these products?"**

> We studied public products as part of competitor research. Our sources, examples, wording and implementation must be independently authored or used with the required licence and attribution. We claim our specific implementation and evidence, not ownership of common library, quiz or report patterns.

Before using that answer, Communication checks the actual asset/source ledger; do not certify originality of an unchecked asset merely from this script.

**"What prevents a competitor adding the same feature?"**

> They could add similar features. We do not claim a permanent technical barrier. The present work is a specific implementation and validation for Hong Kong work-entry requests; reusable source stewardship, reviewed scenarios and evidence of task value are what we need to build further.
