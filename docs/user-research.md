# OfferAnchor: Student Discovery and Task Sessions

Baseline **2.2**; scope revision **11 October 2026**; original planning date **5 October 2026**. Design and Communication lead discovery/sessions; DASE maintains measurement. DASE and Communication independently annotate scenarios; Design adjudicates. AI/DS has development access only until release freeze. No real student-product sessions have been completed.

## Product audience versus the first study

D20 broadens intended eligibility to Hong Kong jobseekers, part-time applicants and new entrants. This protocol intentionally remains a first-cohort student/recent-applicant study; its planned sample and allocation are unchanged. Record each participant's actual background without extrapolating to all workers. Later occupational/age cohorts need a separate protocol and recruitment access. No new local-contact or real-victim prerequisite is introduced. Current tasks assess the financial/account core, not general privacy or labour-law advice.

## Purpose and reachable participants

Find when a student would seek a second check, what selected evidence they can provide, how they check today and whether a short independent question is useful. Fraud prevalence does not establish voluntary use of this workflow.

Recruit consenting classmates/recent internship or part-time applicants if feasible. Include varied experience/language and local/non-local students without claiming representativeness. Victim status, full private chats and institutional data are neither required nor sought. A fictional brief may be reviewed separately by a peer or practitioner; report the recipient's actual role, not professional validation by implication.

## Invitation, consent and data handling

Explain student-prototype research using fictional recruitment instructions, voluntary participation, no real money/credentials/contact, expected 15-25 minutes, optional anonymous notes and the right to stop/withdraw before aggregation. No grade or employment consequences. Check applicable university/ethics requirements before collection; this project does not claim approval.

Obtain explicit consent for the collected material. Use participant IDs P01-P08; contact/consent details stay separate in ignored private storage. Default no recording. Record agreed access/retention/deletion before sessions; proposed private-note retention is 30 days after the final, then deletion unless another justified period was explicitly agreed. Aggregated non-identifiable counts may remain. Project defaults are not legal certification.

The invitation can describe research on understanding job instructions without giving the specific risky interpretation. Participants know messages are fictional; do not present a staged recruiter or paid transaction as real.

## Before-product what-next observation

Run before the interface tutorial or financial warning is explained. Present one separate fictional message and ask: **What would you do next, and why?** Let the participant describe their existing approach before showing OfferAnchor or listing warning signs.

Next offer a neutral link to a selected-message check, alongside the participant's usual options. Record initial choice, reason, whether they choose the offered check, time to submit a first selected instruction, assistance, missing optional context and abandonment. Do not require an advertisement/offer to begin.

This is only unprimed with respect to the product explanation: consent/research context can still affect behavior. A tool offered in a task is not natural discovery. Report observed choices/intake friction; never call them a conversion rate for Hong Kong students or evidence of loss prevention.

Use a separate worksheet row with condition WHAT_NEXT and the relevant fields in [student-tasks-template.csv](../evals/student/student-tasks-template.csv). Task-rubric fields are NA for this observation. Its scenario must not be reused in the paired comparison.

## Open discovery questions

1. Which recent application/onboarding instruction was hardest to understand?
2. What do you normally do to check the sender or request?
3. What would make you pause and open a separate tool before acting?
4. Can you locate the latest message without exporting a whole conversation?
5. Would optional advert/offer context be easy to find, and worth the effort?
6. Who could independently help, and what information would they need?
7. What would make you avoid submission or trust an output too much?
8. Would a strong general AI or an official checklist already meet this need?

Ask before pitching the solution. Record actual answers; AI-created personas/interviews are not participants. Do not request banking OTPs/passwords/full account or ID numbers.

## Paired task session

Use the fixed OA/GAI two-task allocations in [evaluation.md](evaluation.md). A different matched story is used for each tool; family/order assignment is set before outcomes. Provide the strong general-AI prompt and the same reviewed resource packet, preferably the same base model.

After the what-next observation, demonstrate controls with an unrelated practice case. Do not supply the financial answer. Ask the participant to prepare a short independent-verification question/brief identifying the action/resource, evidence, claim/prohibition and unresolved point. They may inspect/edit source text, correct an interpretation or add fictional context/reply as specified by the task. Do not contact anyone.

Start/end/elapsed/interruptions follow the evaluation protocol's fixed five-minute cap. Log every assistance and tool failure. After completion ask about confusion/effort; approval of appearance or intention to use is not performance evidence.

Each export keeps locked provenance/uncertainty/claim-boundary text. For general AI, record what the baseline actually produces and any missing boundary; do not manually improve its scored answer. Private session records may retain synthetic outputs for a second scorer.

## Records, scoring and absence of evidence

The CSV records scenario/family/condition/order, device/browser/language, build/config IDs, timestamps, six rubric scores, unsafe flag, assistance, abandonment, omissions, feedback and withdrawal status. WHAT_NEXT rows additionally record initial action, voluntary offered-tool choice and intake time. Definitions live in [evaluation materials](../evals/student/README.md); blanks do not mean successful completion.

Publish actual paired counts/scores/medians and limitations. Peer feedback and professional review remain separate evidence. Keep identity, voice/video and private messages out of Git.

No participants: report desk research and pending user validation. Fewer than target: publish actual assignments and missing cells; do not substitute synthetic interviews. The runnable app can be built while recruitment is pending, but genuine task usefulness remains a pre-submission evidence priority.
